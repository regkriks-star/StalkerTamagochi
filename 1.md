Research Report: S.T.A.L.K.E.R. & Tamagotchi Mechanics Integration
This research covers core mechanics from both the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. game universe (Anomalies, Artifacts, Detectors, Factions, Emissions) and Tamagotchi evolution systems (Care Mistakes, Discipline, Stats, Branching Growth).
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📄 Research & Lore Overview
S.T.A.L.K.E.R. Game Mechanics
Anomalies & Detectors: Detectors (Response, Bear, Veles, Svarog) allow stalkers to locate artifacts near dangerous anomaly fields (Electro, Burner, Springboard, Gas). Higher-tier detectors reveal exact positions on PDA/KPK screens.
Artifact Trade-offs: Artifacts bestow powerful passive traits (healing, stamina, carry weight) but emit radiation or psychic stress requiring containers or countermeasures.
Emissions & Psy-Storms: Periodic Zone emissions alter anomaly locations, refresh artifact spawns, and force stalkers into shelter within a short countdown window.
Factions & Alignment: Stalkers shift allegiance between Loner, Duty, Freedom, Mercenary, Ecologist, and Monolith depending on choices, reputation, and combat style.
Tamagotchi Evolution & Care Mechanics
Care Mistakes: Triggered when Hunger or Happiness hits zero and the device calls out, but isn't addressed within 15 minutes.
Branching Growth: Cumulative care mistakes, discipline levels, and specific care behaviors dictate which adult character a Tamagotchi evolves into.
Discipline & Status: Regular attention and proper responses prevent delinquent/unhealthy evolution branches.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key Themes for STALKER-Tamagotchi Game Design
Stat-Driven Faction Progression: Replacing standard Tamagotchi evolution with S.T.A.L.K.E.R. factions based on care quality, combat vs. research focus, and radiation/psy management.
Real-time Hazard Response: Linking Tamagotchi care alerts to Zone events like Emissions, anomaly hazards, and equipment maintenance.
Equipable Artifact Synergies: Translating Tamagotchi item/accessory mechanics into active artifact slots with positive and negative stat trade-offs.
